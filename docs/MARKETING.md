# Marketing

Living marketing plan for Peoplearound. Started 2026-08-04.

**Positioning in one line:** the place where neighbors turn ideas into things that exist — with a named, credited team.

**Tagline:** *"Let's do something together."* — the brand promise in five words:
warm, plural, action-first. Decided 2026-08-10.

**Call to action:** *"Start something within your communities."* — the
imperative form for buttons, the app-store subtitle, and hero sub-lines.
"Start" is the product's core verb: founders start, neighbors join. (Copy
variant worth A/B-ing: "…with the people around you," which echoes the brand
name and reads warmer than "communities.")

**The core question:** *"What do you want to do today with your communities?"*
— the app's front door and the idea-composer prompt. It makes the product's
one social contract structural: every surface asks what you want to **do**,
never who you want to meet (see CONCEPT.md § Non-goals). "Today" invites the
small stuff — hangouts, events, one-off asks — which is what gives a
projects app a daily reason to be opened.

## 1. Sports wedge

Sports clubs are the ideal first wedge: they are amateur-organized, hyperlocal,
venue-hungry, recurring (weekly cadence = weekly retention), and chronically
under-tooled (group chats and paper signup sheets). The strategy is to ride the
fastest-growing sports and partner with the associations behind them.

### Top 10 fastest-growing US sports, ranked by fit

Data: SFIA 2026 Topline Participation Report (covers 2025) and NFHS surveys.
2025 was a record year — 250M Americans did at least one sport/fitness

### The pitch to associations

Never "promote our app." Pitch **free infrastructure for their weakest layer**:
national bodies are good at sanctioning and tournaments, bad at
neighborhood-level organizing. Offer:

- a free community page per club (kind: `hobby`/`interest`),
- their local events surfaced on `/events`,
- their ambassadors/members visible on `/people`,
- permanent, confirmed attribution for people who build things (courts,
  leagues, clinics) — a volunteer-recognition system they don't have to build.

Best first door: **USA Pickleball ambassadors** — volunteers whose entire role
is what Peoplearound facilitates.

## 2. Campaigns

- **"Build a court"** — seed 3–5 real projects in launch neighborhoods
  (pickleball court conversion, disc golf course, field cleanup) and document
  them start-to-finish. The finished court with an attributed team page is the
  best possible ad.
- **World Cup afterglow (time-sensitive, Aug–Sep 2026)** — "start a pickup
  soccer group" push while interest peaks.
- **Run club starter kit** — `/start` landing page (shipped) + social
  clips: "Start your neighborhood's run club in 10 minutes."

## 3. Evergreen channels

- **Density-first launches** — one neighborhood at a time to ~50 actives
  before opening the next (Nextdoor playbook). Frontier locations + Founding
  Neighbors (first-10 status, invite attribution) are built for exactly this;
  lead outreach with the founding scarcity.
- **Local PR** — every completed project is a "neighbors built X" story for
  local papers and neighborhood newsletters, with named locals.
- **Organizer migration** — one-page pitch for people already running Facebook
  Groups / WhatsApp chats / Buy Nothing groups: discovery, real rosters,
  events, attribution, no algorithm.
- **Hyperlocal SEO** — "pickleball in {neighborhood}", "{neighborhood}
  projects" landing pages generated from frontier location data; near-zero
  competition on these queries.
- **Non-sports anchors** — libraries, community gardens, PTAs, mutual-aid
  groups; same organizing need, legitimizes the platform beyond sports.
- **Shareable badge moments** — auto-generated OG-card images for badge
  unlocks so every earned badge is a social post linking back to the
  neighborhood.

## 4. Assets

- `/start` — public "start a club" landing page (run club / pickup soccer /
  pickleball starter plans). Shareable while logged out.
- `/invite` — personal invite links with attribution (`?via=<user id>`).
- Badges + confetti celebration (see `INCENTIVES.md`) — recognition loop.
